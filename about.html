ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Career Orientation Of Youth

🎓 Our Objective

The main objective of this project is to help youth understand different career opportunities and choose a suitable career direction according to their interests and skills.

💡 Why This Website?

Many students are confused about career choices. This website provides information about career fields, important skills and a simple career suggestion test.

🚀 Future Scope

In the future, this project can include AI-based career recommendations, courses, internships, job opportunities and personalized career roadmaps.
